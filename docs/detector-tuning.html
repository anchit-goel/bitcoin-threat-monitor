Attribution

What each change was worth

Every threshold was set by measuring against ground_truth.json, not by taste. Each fix below is a single change with a measured before and after.

peel_chain
9% → 100%

Require the forwarded-to wallet to be freshly funded — exactly one wallet paying into it.

Real peel chains create a new wallet at every hop, so each destination has exactly one funder. The 321 clean wallets tripping the rule were forwarding into established hubs with 8 to 162 funders. One condition separated the two populations completely.

structuring
8% → 94%

Require the amounts to crowd the threshold — at least 65% of it — and to reach four or more distinct recipients.

As written, the rule asked only for sub-threshold payments summing over the limit in 24 hours. Any wallet moving $500 a dozen times a day satisfies that. What actually marks structuring is that the payments crowd the limit: someone avoiding a $10,000 report sends $9,400, not $500.

round_trip
12% → 100%

Cap the horizon at 2 days instead of 14, and require two intermediaries.

Over a fortnight, ordinary traffic in a graph this dense throws up value-coherent cycles by chance. Every false positive spanned 90 to 330 hours; every planted wash loop closed in under 40 minutes. And every false positive was two hops — a → b → a, which is a refund, not a wash. The two populations did not overlap on either axis.

rapid_layering
92% → 92%

Left alone.

It was already the strongest of the four and nothing in the measurement argued for touching it. Recording that is part of the result.

Known gap

The eight wallets that take a mixer’s skim off a round trip are not detected. They receive one small payment and never spend it, so nothing about their own behaviour is anomalous — they are reachable only through the ring. This is the whole of the remaining 7% of recall.

Corrections

Three places the spec was wrong

  • The peel-chain condition described a fan-out. “In-degree 1 and out-degree > 5 within ten minutes” is a measure of width. A peel chain has out-degree 2 at every hop and is recognisable by its length. Built literally, the rule would have missed every chain the generator plants. Both conditions are implemented, with the chain walk first.
  • The structuring threshold is fiat, not BTC. Reporting thresholds are denominated in dollars; threshold=10000 read as BTC would never fire on any real dataset. Conversion runs through one shared constant.
  • Detectors need to report counterparties, not just operators. A structuring source without its twenty destinations is half a case, and those destinations are invisible on their own behaviour — one payment in, nothing out, by design. Adding this took fanout recall from 5% to 100%.
Found by tests

Two bugs worth naming

  • A 475% round trip. The returning amount was bounded only from below, so 0.004 BTC leaving and an unrelated 0.019 BTC arriving counted as the same money coming home. Similarity is now symmetric — min/max, capped at 1.
  • Unbounded recursion on single-funder chains. The structuring recipient check walked from a wallet to its funder to its funder, and a peel chain is precisely such a run. It recursed until the interpreter gave up.

Both are now regression-tested. The suite is 17 tests over a hand-built graph carrying one clean instance of each pattern, plus clean wallets that must stay clean.

Method

How to re-run this

The generator plants known patterns and records them in data/ground_truth.json, so precision and recall are measurable rather than argued. If you change a threshold in domain_rules.py, re-measure against that file — do not eyeball the result. The numbers on this page came from a seeded dataset and reproduce exactly.

python -m app.services.data_generator
python -m app.services.graph_builder
pytest tests/ -q
